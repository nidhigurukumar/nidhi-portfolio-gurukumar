Healthcare AI · Project in progress

TrialBridge AI — Agentic Clinical Trial Intelligence

PythonLangGraphAgentic RAGNLPFastAPIQdrant

I'm excited to be working on TrialBridge AI, a healthcare AI project focused on making clinical trial discovery and eligibility screening easier for oncology research teams.

I'm exploring how multi-agent AI and RAG can go beyond simply matching patients with clinical trials—by identifying missing medical information, tracking changes in trial requirements, and helping research teams revisit potential matches as new information becomes available.

What interests me most about this project is the opportunity to apply AI to a real healthcare challenge, making clinical research workflows more efficient, transparent, and accessible.
